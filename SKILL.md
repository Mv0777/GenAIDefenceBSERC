# One-Page Revision Sheet Skill (`/one-page-revision`)

## Goal
Transform raw study notes or a chapter topic into a crisp, high-yield one-page exam revision sheet.

## Instructions
1. **Core Definitions & Intuition (Max 5 bullets):** Summarize the foundational concepts in plain, precise technical language.
2. **Essential Formulas & Laws:** Present every governing equation in clean LaTeX (`$$...$$`) along with variable units and boundary conditions.
3. **Comparison / Trade-off Table:** Include a compact Markdown table contrasting key variants, algorithms, or architectures from the chapter.
4. **Common Exam Pitfalls:** List 3 frequent mistakes students make on numericals or derivations.
5. **3 Rapid-Fire Viva Questions:** End with 3 short conceptual questions (with hidden/concise answers) to test recall.

## Example Comparison (Before vs. After Skill)
- **Without Skill:** Produces a long, unstructured 800-word prose summary that is hard to scan 15 minutes before an exam.
- **With `/one-page-revision` Skill:** Outputs a scannable, single-page formula + trade-off + viva sheet tailored for rapid last-minute revision.
